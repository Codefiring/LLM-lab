facial landmark detection aims to localize facial landmark points on human faces

Driver fuzzing aims to automatically discover security flaws in device drivers by generating and mutating driver interaction sequences.

1. Serves as low-level kernel security validation for hardware-facing components
2. An essential step for product reliability, vulnerability discovery, and crash prevention
3. Widely related to Samsung research and products such as smartphones, Android kernels, and on-device AI / media subsystems
